About me

I’m a master’s student at California State Polytechnic University, Pomona, pursuing an MS in Business Analytics. I also hold an undergraduate degree in Technology and Operations Management.

My professional background is rooted in operations management and manufacturing. I grew up around my family’s furniture manufacturing business and have been exposed to the industry since I was a child. This early experience sparked my passion for furniture design, craftsmanship, and understanding how each piece is constructed.

Over the years, I have worked alongside my father while also gaining experience outside the family business from small, family-owned manufacturers to large-scale furniture manufacturers. These experiences expanded my knowledge of manufacturing operations and encouraged me to formalize my expertise through my studies in technology and operations management.

As my interest in business analytics grew, I began pursuing a master’s degree to strengthen my ability to use data, technology, and analytical thinking to improve business operations. Today, I am passionate about data analysis, process improvement, and modern technologies that help businesses become more efficient and innovative.

My long-term goal is to build a business of my own and modernize my family’s furniture business through a technically informed approach to furniture design, manufacturing, and operations.
